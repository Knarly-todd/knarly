Connect Spotify

Your music. Your rules.

Stop letting algorithms decide what you listen to.

Connect your Spotify account and take control of your music discovery. Explore different genres, venture into neighboring styles, dig into lesser-known tracks, or turn up the hits. You decide where the music goes.

Knarly picks the songs and the Spotify app on your phone or computer plays them. It needs Spotify Premium.

  1. In your app at developer.spotify.com/dashboard, open Settings, then Edit.
  2. Add this exact address as a Redirect URI and save:
  3. Your Client ID is already filled in below.

Where Spotify plays

Knarly sends songs to the Spotify app. If your device isn’t listed, open Spotify on it, play any song for a moment, then press Refresh.

Open Spotify

Library

Stations

Name each station with a genre. Type any name you like, or pick one from the list.

Where songs come from

Tag a playlist with a genre and every song in it joins that station. Spotify only lets apps read playlists you made or collaborate on.

Sort by artist

Give an artist a genre once and all their songs follow. Add a second genre for artists who fit two stations, like pop rock on both Pop and Rock.

Automatic star ratings

Spotify doesn’t share stream counts with apps, so Knarly rates songs from Last.fm’s public play counts instead. Stars you tap yourself always win.

Play gaps

About how often one song can come back, at the Balanced popularity setting. The Popularity dial stretches or shrinks these.

Back up and restore

Saves your station names, genre tags, playlists, ratings and stream log to a file, so you can move them to another browser or window.
